Attractions

Meet the thrilling attractions that go beyond standard sightseeing, experience the excitement of Singapore after dark. From neon-lit go-karts to night safaris, escape rooms, and late-night arcade fun, this section showcases nighttime attractions ideal just for you.

XCAPE Singapore

Jump into the world of most immersive games, love to solve puzzles ? This escape room place is for you.

Night Safari Tour

Explore the worlds first ever nocturnal wildlife park, where you will be see exotic animals up-close at night.

Hyperdrive

Singapore's first ever indoor go-kart, the top adrenaline activity for adventurous individuals.

Gardens By The Bay

The nature that transforms into a glowing fantasy at night. One of the citys greatest sight-seeing spot.

MBS Spectra Light Show

Marina Bay Sands signature outdoor light and water show that transforms the bay of Singapore night life.

Cow Play Cow Moo

The best interaction and retro games for you, all night long at Cow Play Cow Moo, one of the biggest arcades in the city.

Cruise by the River Tour

Offering the citys greatest bay scenery, catch the river tour at the Singapore Clarke Quay.
